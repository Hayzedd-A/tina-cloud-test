At Gourmet Twist, every loaf has a story. The banana bread you unwrap today started as a single recipe, baked in a home kitchen in Lekki with nothing but ripe bananas, real butter, and a stubborn refusal to settle for ordinary.

## From one recipe to 30+ flavours

The original Gourmet Twist banana bread was simple: moist, dense, and rich with banana flavour. But customers kept asking — what if you added chocolate? What about Nutella? Coconut? So we did. Then we went further: Double Chocolate, Triple Choc, Baileys-infused, Banana x Coconut, Nutty Mix, and more. Each new flavour had to pass one test: it had to be noticeably better than anything else you could find in Lagos.

## What makes it different

Three things that we never compromise on:

**Fresh every day.** Every loaf is baked the morning it's delivered. We don't freeze and we don't reheat. If it isn't baked today, it isn't delivered today.

**Real ingredients.** No artificial flavour substitutes. The chocolate in our Triple Choc is real chocolate. The Baileys is real Baileys. The coconut is real coconut.

**The texture.** Getting banana bread right is harder than it looks. Too dry and it crumbles; too wet and it collapses. After hundreds of test batches, we landed on a formula that gives you a crust that cracks when you cut it and a crumb that stays moist for days.

## 176,000 reasons to keep going

The number we're most proud of isn't any flavour count — it's the 176,000 people who've chosen Gourmet Twist. Every order is a vote of trust, and we don't take that lightly. Which is why the recipe gets refined, never shortcuts.
