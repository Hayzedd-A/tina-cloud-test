A banana bread gift from Gourmet Twist lands differently to a box of chocolates or a generic hamper. It says "I thought about this." But with 30+ flavours on the menu, the question we get most often is: which one do I pick?

Here's a simple guide.

## For the chocolate lover

Go **Triple Choc** or **Double Chocolate**. Both are deeply rich — Triple Choc layers chocolate batter, chips, and a ganache swirl; Double Chocolate is more restrained but equally satisfying. If they're a true chocolate obsessive, Triple Choc. If you're not sure how intense they like it, Double Chocolate is the safer bet.

## For someone who likes things a little different

**Baileys-Infused** is a crowd-pleaser for adults who appreciate something with a twist. The alcohol bakes off but the flavour stays. It's a great choice for birthdays, promotions, or any occasion where you want to be remembered.

## For the health-conscious friend

**Nutty Mix** — banana bread studded with mixed nuts adds texture and a sense of substance. Pairs well with morning coffee and doesn't feel indulgent in the way a chocolate-heavy loaf might.

## For families with kids

**Nutella Swirl** is a perennial favourite with younger eaters. The sweetness is there but it's balanced by the banana base. Order this for a school run treat or a weekend family breakfast that actually gets finished.

## For the office

When you're feeding a room, variety wins. Order two or three different flavours — Classic, Double Choc, and one wild card — and let people try. You'll be the person who brought the thing everyone talked about.

## Getting it there fresh

All Gourmet Twist orders are baked the morning of delivery. Order by 9pm the night before for same-day delivery on the Island. Mainland orders need an extra day. [Order here](/shop) — the flavour you pick will be baked fresh for the person you're thinking of.
